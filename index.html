About Us

Tiger Strategies Fund is set up as a trust under the Indian Trusts Act, 1882, and Tiger Strategies Fund – I is its scheme. The Fund's vision is to build a diversified financial services platform with capital raising and strategic advisory.

Disclaimer

This website is for information only. It is not an offer, invitation or solicitation to invest in Tiger Strategies Fund – I or any other product.

Tiger Strategies Fund – I is registered with SEBI as a Category I Alternative Investment Fund (Venture Capital Fund). SEBI registration does not guarantee the performance of the Fund or the returns to investors. No returns are assured or guaranteed.

An investment in the Fund is suitable only for sophisticated investors and private investors who are eligible to invest in Category I AIFs under the AIF Regulations. It needs the financial ability and willingness to stay invested for the whole term, and to accept the high risk and lack of liquidity of an investment of this kind.

The Fund is close-ended with a term of 10 years, extendable by up to 2 years subject to the required approvals. In the normal course, no exit option is available to investors until the end of the term.

Units can be offered only by private placement, through the Fund's Private Placement Memorandum, to a limited number of eligible investors. The Private Placement Memorandum has not been approved by SEBI or any other regulatory authority. Losses in the Fund are borne by investors. Read the Private Placement Memorandum and consult your own advisers before investing.
